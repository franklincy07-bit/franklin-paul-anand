Before a non-profit can raise funds or hire staff, it needs the right legal form and registrations. This module explains what they are and why they matter, in plain language.

_Rules change often. Each lesson will show the date it was recorded and link to the official sources. It is general guidance, not legal advice – check your own situation with a chartered accountant or lawyer._
