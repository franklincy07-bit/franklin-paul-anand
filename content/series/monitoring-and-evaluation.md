For more than 15 years I have built monitoring systems and trained programme teams, partners and public policy students to use them. This module brings that teaching together: short, practical and built around real decisions, not jargon.

Each lesson takes about ten minutes to watch. Underneath every video you will find the key points, a worked example and a **Try it yourself** exercise that you can use with your own programme.

This module will cover:

1. **Understanding monitoring** – how it differs from evaluation, and why it matters during implementation.
2. **The results chain** – inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes and impact.
3. **Theory of change** – the "if… then… because" behind a programme, and its assumptions.
4. **From results to indicators** – deciding what to measure at each level.
5. **What makes a good indicator** – and the most common mistakes.
6. **A monitoring system people actually use** – who collects what, how often, and who acts on it.
7. **Evaluation** – its purpose, the main types, and when each is worth the cost.
